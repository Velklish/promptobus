# PB-37.4 · The two shipped Gemini promotion citations carry the list blend as figure, so promotion-expired prints the same number twice and never names the promotional one

- **Order:** 75
- **Scope:** `models/catalog.json` (the `quotaCost` citations of `cursor-gemini-38-high` and `cursor-gemini-37-high`), `test/model-routing-catalog.test.mjs`
- **Created:** 2026-09-09
- **Dependencies:** none

## Context

Finding of the PB-125 review (isolated reviewer, run `pb-run-0909-t20260909-132344`), on the data PB-37.1 shipped. PB-37.1 defined the promotional citation as `figure` = the promotional figure and `listFigure` = the list figure it replaces ([guides/model-routing](../../guides/model-routing.md) § Re-rating a row, step 4), and the `promotion-expired` warning renders both. On the only two shipped rows with a promotion, `figure` still holds the list blend — `"$4.50 per 1M tokens ($1.50 in / $7.50 out list; a 50 % discount is applied today, giving $0.75 / $3.75)"` — and `listFigure` holds `"$4.50 per 1M tokens blended ($1.50 in / $7.50 out list)"`. The warning printed from 2026-09-07 on therefore reads `promotional figure $4.50 …; list figure $4.50 … gives band 2`: the $2.25 promotional blend that band 1 rests on (stated in `evidence.text` and in ADR-005) appears nowhere in it. The PB-37.1 regression synthesizes a complete promotional citation on a row with no promotion and filters its assertions on that row's id, so the two real rows' warning text is never read.

## Work to do

- Set `figure` on both rows to the promotional blend (for example `"$2.25 per 1M tokens blended ($0.75 in / $3.75 out under the 50 % promotion)"`); keep `listFigure` as the $4.50 list blend, `listBand` 2 and `quotaCost` 1 — no re-banding.
- A test that reads the two real rows' `promotion-expired` warnings from the shipped catalog (at a `now` after 2026-09-06) and asserts the promotional and list figures differ and the promotional one names $2.25.

## Out of scope

- The observation-date rule and the validate-only status of the warning — PB-37.1, decided.

## Verification

- The new test is red on the current catalog and green after the two-row edit; `models validate` prints the two warnings with distinct figures.
