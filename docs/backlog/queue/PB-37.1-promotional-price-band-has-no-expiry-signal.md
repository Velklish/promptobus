# PB-37.1 · A quotaCost band resting on a promotion has no expiry signal, and nothing goes red when the promotion ends

- **Order:** 30
- **Scope:** [models/catalog.json](../../../models/catalog.json), `lib/model-routing/validate.js` (`staleWarnings`), [guides/model-routing.md](../../guides/model-routing.md) § Re-rating a row
- **Created:** 2026-09-06
- **Dependencies:** PB-37 (the anchor pairs and the promotion rule it fixed)

## Context

[ADR-005](../../adr/adr-005-ten-point-scale-absolute-bands-calibrate.md) fixed the `quotaCost` anchor pair on **list** prices — $2.50 → 1 and $30.00 → 10 — and allows a **promotion** to move one model's own figure while the cited promotion is in force. Two shipped rows use that allowance today:

```text
$ node -e "const c=require('./models/catalog.json');for(const t of c.tuples)\
if(/promotion/.test(t.evidence?.text??''))console.log(t.id, t.ratings.quotaCost)"
cursor-gemini-38-high 1
cursor-gemini-37-high 1
```

Each row's `evidence.text` names the list blend ($4.50, `$1.50 in / $7.50 out`), the band the list price would give (**2**), the promotion, and the date it was seen in force (2026-09-06). So the row states what it should become — but only in prose.

**Nothing fires on the day the promotion ends.** The only calendar signal the catalog has is `stale-rating`, which `validate` raises from `assessedAt` at `STALE_RATING_DAYS` (90, `lib/model-routing/catalog.js`). A promotion can end well inside 90 days, and when it does both rows go on scoring `quotaCost: 1` — a full band cheaper than their list price supports — with `models validate` green. Under `economy` and inside `balance`'s spend penalty that is a real thumb on the scale: `spendPenalty = balance.spendUnit × (quotaCost − 1) / 9` is exactly zero at band 1 and `spendUnit / 9` at band 2.

The band itself is not the defect — the owner's decision is that a promotion applies to the model's own figure while it holds. The defect is that "while it holds" is a claim no check can see.

## Work to do

- Decide where the expiry lives. The cheapest shape is a dated field on the citation the row already carries — a `validUntil` beside `figure` in the `quotaCost` source, or a `promotionUntil` on `evidence` — so the fact is machine-readable rather than only written in `evidence.text`.
- `models validate` warns when a row's promotion window has passed, naming the row, the promotional figure, the list figure and the band the list price gives. A warning and not an error: the catalog is still loadable and the person re-bands on their own schedule, which is how `stale-rating` already behaves.
- The schema and the hand-written grammar both learn the field (they are held to one grammar by the parity check in `test/model-routing-catalog.test.mjs`).
- [guides/model-routing.md](../../guides/model-routing.md) § Re-rating a row gains the sentence: a promotional figure is cited with the date it expires, or it is not cited.

## Out of scope

- Fetching prices or checking a promotion against a vendor page. The catalog is a hand-maintained document and this is a reminder, not a feed.
- Re-banding the two rows now: on 2026-09-06 the promotion was in force, and the band is correct as of the assessment date.

## Verification

- A catalog row whose promotion date has passed produces the warning and `validate` still returns `ok`; one whose date is in the future produces nothing.
- The parity check stays green with the new field in both descriptions.
