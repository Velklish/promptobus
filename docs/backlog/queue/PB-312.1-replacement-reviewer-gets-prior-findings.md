# PB-312.1 · A reviewer raised in place of a dead one gets the dead reviewer's last findings

- **Order:** 220
- **Scope:** [03-cli § Review](../../reference/03-cli.md), `lib/review.js`, `skills/solo-review/SKILL.md`
- **Created:** 2026-09-30
- **Cost:** major
- **Dependencies:** none

## Context

Finding discovered while working on PB-312.
Evidence: a repeat `review` on a dead reviewer session starts a new reviewer with the full prompt, and nothing of the earlier findings reaches it:

<!-- quote:../../../lib/review.js -->
    warn(`starting a new reviewer with the full prompt: prior findings left with the session, it looks at the diff from a clean slate`);
<!-- /quote -->

After PB-312 the reviewer confirms the closure of its own findings on its next pass, and the orchestrator must not confirm them itself. On this path nobody confirms them: a wrong "fixed" from the worker passes unless the new reviewer finds the same defect again on its own. The skills name the gap in words (`skills/solo-review/SKILL.md`, "Collect the report", step 5). A message with the findings sent right after the lift races the new session's first turn. Found by the PB-312 review, 2026-09-30.

## Work to do

- `review` on a dead reviewer puts the dead reviewer's last `result` into the replacement's prompt, marked as findings to check for closure first.
- The skills drop the gap sentence once the prompt carries the findings.

## Out of scope

- Keeping a dead session alive, or reviving it.

## Verification

- A test with a dead reviewer record and a prior `result`: the replacement prompt quotes that `result`; without a prior `result` the prompt is unchanged.
