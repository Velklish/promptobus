# PB-37.2 · `done` reads a sixty-second cache for the end-of-run window value, so most records reach `calibrate` with no spend evidence

- **Order:** 40
- **Scope:** `lib/model-routing/telemetry.js` (`endReader`), `lib/done.js`, [reference/03-cli.md](../../reference/03-cli.md) § Participant telemetry
- **Created:** 2026-09-06
- **Dependencies:** PB-36 (the record), PB-37 (`models calibrate`, the reader that makes the cost visible)

## Context

A telemetry record's `windows[].usedPercentAtEnd` is read out of the availability cache as it stands at `done`, and **only while that entry may still be used** — `endReader` calls the same `entryLive` the resolver decides on, so a stale entry gives `null` for every window (`lib/model-routing/telemetry.js`). The window TTL is sixty seconds. `done` probes nothing.

So the end reading survives only when someone ran `promptobus models --refresh` inside the minute before the close.

**The workaround is already written down in three places, and it works.** `skills/orchestrate` § "Refresh right before you close" gives the orchestrator the two commands and the words "in that order, back to back"; `skills/solo-review` says the same at the close of a review; and [reference/03-cli.md](../../reference/03-cli.md) § Participant telemetry states it as the rule:

> **run `promptobus models --refresh` right before `promptobus done` to record the end value; otherwise the record carries the start value only.**

Both things are true at once, and the finding is the first of them: **the mechanism has a gap, and a skill instruction is what covers it.** An agent that follows the skill records the value; a person closing a task by hand, an older skill, a consumer with skills of its own, or an agent that simply closed the task without reading that section does not — and nothing tells any of them at the time. A rule that must be remembered on every close, by every caller, is a workaround; whether the mechanism should stop needing it is the decision below.

[ADR-005](../../adr/adr-005-ten-point-scale-absolute-bands-calibrate.md) deliberately left the decision to this pass — 03-cli said "whether to pay it is a decision the calibration pass gets to make" — and **this pass declined to add a probe**: `models calibrate` reports how many of a key's runs carried no usable window instead of paying the preflight budget on the path that closes a run. That is a defensible answer and it is not a free one, which is why it is filed rather than closed.

What it costs, concretely: `quotaCost` is one of the two ratings `calibrate` proposes, and its only evidence is the window delta. A key whose runs all closed without a refresh reaches the report with `median window delta — pp over 0 sample(s)` and the line `quotaCost: no proposal — no usable window delta on this key or on the pivot`. Half the command's output depends on a step the caller has to remember, and the mechanism says nothing when it was not taken: the omission is silent at the close and only shows up later, in a report about runs that are already over.

This is a hypothesis about frequency, not a measurement: no count of the owner's own records was taken (a suite must not read a person's telemetry file, and this worker did not read it by hand either). What is verified is the mechanism — the TTL, the `entryLive` call, and the absence of a probe in `done`.

## Work to do

- Decide whether `done` should refresh the windows of the harnesses its participants ran on, and if so under what bound. The shapes worth weighing:
  - a flag (`promptobus done --refresh`) — explicit, and costs nothing to a person who does not want it, but is the same manual step under a shorter name;
  - a refresh of only the harnesses with a record to write, with a small budget of its own and a failure that costs the end reading and nothing else;
  - a line printed by `done` when a record was written with no end value, so the omission is at least visible at the time rather than only in a later report.
- Whichever is chosen, the three places that carry the workaround today change with it: `skills/orchestrate` § "Refresh right before you close", `skills/solo-review` § the close, and the reference sentence quoted above. If the answer is that the instruction stays as it is, that is a decision too: the finding closes as rejected with the reason recorded, so the next reader knows it was weighed rather than missed.

## Out of scope

- Changing what `calibrate` does with a missing delta: reporting it and omitting the rating is correct however the record was written.
- Any probe on a path that is not `done`.

## Verification

- A `done` with the chosen behaviour writes a record whose `usedPercentAtEnd` is present without a preceding `models --refresh`, on a stub adapter, and the close still finishes when the probe refuses.
