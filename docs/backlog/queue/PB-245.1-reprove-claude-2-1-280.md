## Context

Finding discovered while working on PB-245.
Evidence: `claude --version` → `2.1.280 (Claude Code)` on 2026-09-23, after the owner's `claude update` from 2.1.263, while the driver still records the older proof:

<!-- quote:../../../lib/driver-claude.js -->
export const PROVEN_CLAUDE_VERSION = '2.1.263';
<!-- /quote -->

The 2.1.280 baked table points the alias at the successor — `opus.default` and `latest_per_family.opus` are both `claude-opus-5-5` — and the driver's alias table follows the proven binary, so it still reads:

<!-- quote:../../../lib/driver-claude.js -->
  opus: ['claude-opus-5'],
<!-- /quote -->

A lift with `--model opus` — and every lift without `--model`, since `DEFAULT_MODEL` is `opus` — starts Opus 5.5 and is recorded against Opus 5's rows by `models calibrate`. The warden's injection into a live session's socket is also unobserved on 2.1.280; a refusal there only delays a participant until it calls `mailbox` itself.
