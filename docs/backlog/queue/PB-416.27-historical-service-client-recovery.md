## Context

Evidence: private workspace consumer smoke on 2026-11-13, Promptobus 0.32.9 through consumer Host 0.107.0. The retained original command log is `/private/tmp/promptobus-smoke-20261113/commands.md`; the run summary is `report.md` in that directory. Public shared-service startup exited 1 with `Live clients belong to another shared service runtime; close and reconnect them before upgrade`. Retained discovery named consumer Host 0.106.0. Status reported the previous service not running; its recorded PID was absent. A historical client's managed launch record returned `ENOENT`, leaving its lifetime unknown.

No matching confirmed Stop receipt for that historical client was established in this smoke. An absent service PID or launch record does not prove native termination. The successful test used a separate supported service home; it did not repair or qualify the default home.

PB-416.23 already implements exact receipt-backed retirement, and PB-405 covers dead-native task closure. This finding must first check those supported routes against the retained historical case. The observed refusal is valid fail-closed behavior; it is not evidence that either accepted implementation is broken.

## Acceptance criteria

- The retained case has an evidence-backed disposition: recovered through a supported owned route, or explicitly unresolved with the exact missing proof and required owner action.
- A matching confirmed receipt remains eligible for the existing retirement route; absent, mismatched or live evidence remains unknown and blocks unsafe retirement.
- The default service home is tested separately from an isolated smoke home. Isolated success is never reported as recovery of historical clients.

## Out of scope

- Automatically deleting historical clients, treating `ENOENT` as terminal proof, force-killing processes, claiming another session's identity, or changing unrelated service homes.
- Reimplementing PB-416.23 or the zero-client task/watch lifetime issue in PB-416.26.
