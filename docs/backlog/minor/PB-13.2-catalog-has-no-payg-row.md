# PB-13.2 · No pay-as-you-go tuple ships, so the PAYG filter has nothing to act on

- **Scope:** [03-cli](../../reference/03-cli.md)
- **Created:** 2026-09-05
- **Dependencies:** PB-13, PB-18
- **Cost:** minor

## Context

Finding from PB-13. All forty-five tuples in `models/catalog.json` are `"billing": "subscription"` and all one hundred and thirty-five price fields are `null`:

```text
$ node -e "const c=require('./models/catalog.json');console.log(c.tuples.filter(t=>t.billing==='payg').length)"
0
```

That is the honest state, not an omission. The three accounts the drivers log into are subscriptions, and PB-13's rule is that a price goes in only from a public documented price list that can be named — nothing was invented to fill the field.

The consequence is that three parts of the contract ship with no shipped row to act on: the `--allow-payg` flag, the `payg.allow` overlay key, and the `payg-not-allowed` exclusion code. The resolver path is still testable — `test/fixtures/model-routing/catalog.json` carries `other-metered` as a `payg` row — so PB-18 is not blocked, and this entry is not a defect report. It is the note that the money-cost half of the catalog is unexercised by real data, and that whoever adds the first pay-as-you-go row is the first person to find out whether the price fields carry what a decision needs.

## Work to do

- When a pay-as-you-go tuple becomes rateable from a named price list, add it, and check on that first real row that `prices` is enough: today nothing reads the price fields at all, and ADR-003 leaves money out of scoring on purpose (decision 1 — PAYG is excluded unless `--allow-payg`).
- Decide whether the reference should say plainly that the shipped catalog has no such row, so a person reading about `--allow-payg` does not go looking for what it would admit. [guides/model-routing.md](../../guides/model-routing.md) says it; [03-cli](../../reference/03-cli.md) does not.

## Out of scope

- Filling a price from anything other than a public documented list.
- Changing decision 1 of ADR-003 — that is a new decision, not a task.

## Verification

- A pay-as-you-go row in `models/catalog.json` that `models validate` accepts, and a run with and without `--allow-payg` that differs in the candidate list by exactly that row.

## Deferred

- **Deferred:** 2026-09-05
- **Reason:** every account the three drivers log into is a subscription, and a PAYG row with an invented price would be worse than none. The resolver's PAYG path stays exercised by the golden fixture. The deferral also said that no price could be cited. That half stopped holding with PB-29 (`de6d9814`, 2026-09-06): vendor list prices are cited as the basis of every `quotaCost` band (`docs/guides/model-routing.md:118`).
- **Return condition:** a harness account billed pay-as-you-go appears among the owner's tools, or a public price list for one of the shipped models can be named — then the row is added with `billing: payg` and its prices.
