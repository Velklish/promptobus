## Context

The 2026-09-12 measurement covered 268 participant sessions. It found 52,748 Claude usage records with 51,341,563 output tokens, 11,933 Codex token-count records with 4,556,184 output tokens across 35 Codex sessions, and 44 Cursor session-store records with no usage field. The figures come from the baseline parsers `codex_parse.py` and `sec12.py`; they are evidence of usage, not a complete throughput observation. Model-active time is missing for Claude and Codex alike, so any tok/s inferred from wall-clock gaps remains an upper bound, not a measurement.
