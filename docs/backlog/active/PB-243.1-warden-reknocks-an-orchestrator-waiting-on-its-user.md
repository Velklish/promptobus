# PB-243.1 · The warden re-knocks every two minutes an orchestrator whose turn is waiting on its user, and each knock lands in its context

- **Scope:** [lib/warden.js](../../../lib/warden.js), [lib/notification.js](../../../lib/notification.js), [reference/04-protocol.md](../../reference/04-protocol.md)
- **Created:** 2026-09-24
- **Dependencies:** none
- **Cost:** major
- **Previous order:** 135
- **Taken:** 2026-09-25

## Context

Measured on 2026-09-24 in a consumer's run (task `queue-0924-t20260923-233651`). The orchestrator's turn stopped twice on a question to its user — a harness tool that waits for a human answer: from about 00:03Z to 04:55Z, and from about 04:57Z to 07:02Z. The mailbox held unread mail both times (7, then 3 messages), and nobody could act on it until the human answered.

The task's `supervisor.log` shows the warden kept knocking through both waits, one knock about every two minutes:

- `awk '$1>="2026-09-24T00:13" && $1<"2026-09-24T04:55" && /notification orchestrator/' supervisor.log | wc -l` → 137;
- the same for `04:58`..`07:03` → 58.

Every knock reached the orchestrator's session as a separate notification ("the mailbox … has unread: 7" / "unread: 3"), and every one stayed in its transcript. A token that enters the transcript is re-read on every later turn (the parent card's measurement), so 195 identical postcards are paid for again on each turn of the rest of the run. The orchestrator could do nothing with any of them: its turn was not over.

The warden is meant not to knock a session that is busy. This session was not idle — its turn was open, waiting on the human — yet it was treated as a reachable idle session for the whole five hours.

## Work to do

- Find what the warden reads as "busy" for a Claude orchestrator and why a turn open on a user question does not count.
- A turn waiting on its user gets no repeat knock, or at most one after the wait ends; the unread mail stays in the mailbox as it does now.
- A test: a session in that state receives no repeat knocks over several warden periods.

## Out of scope

- The first knock for new mail, and the delivery format itself — the parent card's subject.

## Verification

- Replaying the two waits (an orchestrator turn held on a user question with unread mail) produces no more than one knock per wait in `supervisor.log`.
