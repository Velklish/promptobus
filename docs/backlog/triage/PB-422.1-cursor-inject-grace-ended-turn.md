# PB-422.1 · A Cursor session reads busy for the rest of the 20 s injection grace after a turn that already ended

- **Scope:** [Cursor: the persist session](../../reference/05-drivers.md#cursor-the-persist-session)
- **Created:** 2026-11-05
- **Dependencies:** none
- **Cost:** major

## Context

`turnState` in `lib/cursor-persist.ts` at `8a1a546d` counts an injection as a running turn for `INJECT_GRACE_MS` (20 000 ms) whenever the transcript does not show a turn in progress: `fresh = injected within the grace && !seen?.busy`. The constant's own comment gives the reason — the transcript's first line arrived 3.09 and 8.03 s after an injection — but the condition does not tell "the turn has not started yet" from "the turn started and has already ended". A turn that ends inside the grace leaves the session busy for the rest of it, so the warden holds the next delivery and every caller of `inspect` sees a busy session that is idle.

Evidence: 2026-11-05 on `8a1a546d`, official Node 26.13.0, eight cores, `node test/promptobus-mixed.test.mjs` (Cursor stub worker) with the two waits of scenario step 7 timed by a temporary probe: the wait for the worker session to read idle after its second result took 18 445 ms, the following stamp wait 3 ms; the same wait in `promptobus-e2e.test.mjs` (Claude stub) took 102 ms. The stub's turn ends in about two seconds, so the session read busy for the remaining ~18 s of the grace.

## Work to do

- Count an injection as a running turn only until the transcript records activity after it; from then on the transcript decides.
- Keep the grace for a transcript that is silent or absent after the injection.

## Out of scope

- The length of the grace and of `TURN_IDLE_MS`.

## Verification

- A `turnState` verdict: a turn that started and ended after the injection, inside the grace, is not busy; the existing grace verdicts stay green.
- `promptobus-mixed.test.mjs` standalone before and after, by number.
