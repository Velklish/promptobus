# PB-14.3 · Two commands writing the availability cache at once lose the loser's entries

- **Scope:** [03-cli](../../reference/03-cli.md) (§ Availability), `lib/model-routing/cache.js`
- **Created:** 2026-09-05
- **Parent:** PB-14

## Context

Reported by the preflight track's worker on 2026-09-05 and filed by the orchestrator (the worker's branch holds two findings of its own, so this one takes the next number). `lib/model-routing/cache.js` does read-modify-write of `routingPaths().cacheFile` with tmp+rename and no lock. Two commands probing at once — a `spawn` and a `models --refresh` in another terminal — both read the same document and the second rename wins: the loser's entries are lost, not corrupted. The failure mode today is a re-probe on the next run, a cost rather than a wrong decision. The task store has `withTaskLock` (`src/v1`), but the cache is account-scoped and outside the store. Hypothesis until measured: the race window is one preflight, up to 15 s.

## Work to do

- Serialise cache writes across processes — a lock file next to `cacheFile`, or merge-on-write that re-reads before the rename — and say in the reference what happens when two commands probe at once.

## Out of scope

- Locking the task store: it has its own lock.

## Verification

- Two preflights started concurrently with distinct stub harnesses both land in the file; a probe removing the lock loses one of them.
