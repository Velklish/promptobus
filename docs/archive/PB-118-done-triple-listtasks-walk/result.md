# PB-118 · Result

**Closed 2026-09-17, completed.** `done()` now parses the task tree once and hands the same list to all three of its sweeps — the worktree walk and the participant-secrets walk in `lib/done.js`, and journal cleanup through `lib/prune.js`'s `sweepJournals` and `pruneCandidates`. Before it, each of the three called `listTasks(home)` on its own, so every `task.json` under `tasks/` was read, parsed and schema-validated three times per `promptobus done`, and a broken journal was warned about up to three times in one call.

**The visible half of this is the warning, and that is what the new fixture holds.** The parse count is not observable from outside; the repeated warning is. `test/promptobus-done.test.mjs` gained two checks — `a broken task journal is warned about once per done() call, not once per sweep` and `the ordinary closed tasks are untouched by the broken neighbour` — the second because a fixture that only counts warnings passes just as well when the sweep has stopped doing its work.

**The ordering that made the old shape defensible is unchanged and now says so out loud.** The three sweeps run in a fixed order — sessions stopped, then worktrees, then journals — and the shared list is taken before any of them rather than threaded between them, so nothing binds two independent cleanups together. `lib/store.js`'s `withTaskCache`, which the card names as the memoization that exists and is never called, is still never called: sharing one list along the call chain is the narrower repair, and it does not leave a cache whose invalidation nobody owns.
