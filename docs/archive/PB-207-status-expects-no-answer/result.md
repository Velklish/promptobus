# PB-207 · Result

**Closed 2026-09-12.** Done. The protocol reference now states which message types expect an answer and which do not, both preambles say the same thing — a `status` expects none, and a turn must not end with nothing sent since the last thing that asked for one — and the guard's broken promise is repaired from both sides at once: the verdict now really checks that a reply went out, and the text "and reply in your role" became "and answer the types that ask for an answer". The card asked which side was chosen and why; the answer is both, because each was the cheaper fix for a different half of the gap.

**The table is wider than the card's letter, deliberately.** The card names two kinds — a `question` and the hand-over that asks for acceptance. The table names four: `task`, `question`, `review`, `result`; `status`, `answer` and `artifact` expect none. The two extra are load-bearing rather than tidy: of the nine participants PB-203 rests on, those that ended a turn cleanly and sent nothing had exactly one inbound message in their whole life, and it was a `task` or a `review`. At two kinds the gate of PB-203 is dead on six of those nine — on the very evidence it grew from. The widening is recorded in the card so that a later reader narrowing it back knows it would be undoing a decision.

**The card's trap held, and that was checked rather than asserted.** `src/` and `schemas/` are untouched to the line — `git diff` over both is empty and `message.schema.json` is byte-identical — so the header stays a convention inside `body` and no message type was added. The `SILENT` escalation is not touched: it lives in the supervisor, and the work never entered that file. A new check states the opposite side directly: the orchestrator owes nothing by the table and ends its turn cleanly after reading a `result`. There is no mechanical counter of outstanding answers — no field in `health.json`, no byte on disk; the debt is computed where it is needed. The door of the debt is an address rather than a role list, so a role the list has not learned cannot fall out of the state silently.

**Checks.** Gates on the merged tree: `gates` exit 0, **gates 4, green 4** — `npm test` exit 0 (64 of 64 files, 1902 verdicts), `lint` 0, `audit` 0, `pins` 0. A grep for the old promise leaves exactly two occurrences in the repository, and both are cards quoting the old text for the record. Guard tests 68 → 78 verdicts across the branch.

**Left open, and this is the important item.** The card's third check — a run measured after the change showing the count of status-answers dropped, with the before and after named — is **not closed, and no number was invented for it**. Measuring it would have meant measuring the very run the author is a participant of, on a sample of two workers, against a "before" of 1424 recorded waits. The before-figures stay where the card put them; the after belongs to the next full run, taken by the same scripts and the same method. Stating a drop from this run would have been a measurement generalised past its conditions, which is the failure this whole backlog is about.
