# PB-30 · Result

**Closed 2026-09-06.** Completed. `balance` is the fifth `--strategy` value — a choice layer over the `balanced` scoring, not a filter, additive to the four ADR-003 strategies and without a weight set of its own (`WEIGHT_STRATEGIES` names the four an overlay may re-weight). Every scored candidate gains a pace block in percentage points of its binding window — `window`, `usedShare`, `elapsedShare`, `underspend = (elapsedShare − usedShare) × 100`, `spendPenalty = balance.spendUnit × (quotaCost − 1) / 4` read from the role's ratings, `effective`, `eligible` with `no-pace` | `window-spent`, `representative` on the tuple that stands for its harness — computed for every strategy, published only under `balance`. The applicable windows of a tuple are the account-wide ones plus the scope that covers it (`windowBinds` for the three scope shapes, the `api` pool as the complement of the `auto` lists), so `remaining` is per tuple under all five strategies. The pick: each harness represented by its best eligible tuple meeting the role's floor, the largest `effective` leads, harnesses within `balance.band` (5) of the leader tie and the representatives' `balanced` score decides, then ADR-003's tie-break; nothing eligible → the best `balanced` score with `balance-fallback`. Quality floors are policy values `qualityFloor: { worker: 3, reviewer: 5 }` (`reviewerQualityFloor` read as an alias with a `quality-floor-alias` warning when both are stated), choice rules with `reviewer-floor-not-met` and the new `worker-floor-not-met`. The inventory is the snapshot rows with `hidden !== true`. The decision document moves to `schemaVersion` 2 (`strategy` enum, `candidates[].pace`, `strategySource` as a field PB-32 writes, the new codes); `render` prints the pace table with the representative marked. `routingMetadata` gains `windows: [{ id, kind, scope, usedPercent }]` — the chosen tuple's applicable windows at the lift, read by PB-36 at `done`. The glossary's `strategy` row names the fifth value; the three synopses and `lib/cli.js` help too.

The fixture pair `balance-{catalog,snapshot}.json`, validated against the schemas by a test, shows the point: on one snapshot `balanced` picks `claude-fable` (68.05) and `balance` picks `codex-sol` (65.60) — Codex sixteen points behind its weekly pace, Cursor's `auto` pool fourteen ahead of its cycle.

Review (isolated, xhigh): the one major — `spendPenalty` read `tuple.ratings.quotaCost` while the score read the role's ratings, so a `roleRatings.reviewer.quotaCost` could flip the harness; closed by passing `role` into `paceOf`, with a test where the penalty and the score component move together. The representative marker, the `null` fields of `no-pace`, the JSDoc on `now` and the file header, the `04-protocol` metadata list — closed in `1d9aaac` and `e746c97`. Two composition decisions the ADR left implicit are recorded in the reference: the floor applies when the representative is chosen (one stage, no circularity), and the pace block is published only under `balance`.
